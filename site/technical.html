1.0 MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION

Parameter Space & Non-Parametric Estimators

Formalizing the Share of Model Voice (SoMV) and Weighted Visibility Index (WVI) across the sample space.

Sample Space Definition

Let $M = 8$ regional corridors, $Q = 7$ intent prompts, and $E = 5$ engines:

$$Omega = M \times Q \times E, \quad |\Omega| = N = 280$$

Indicator variable for target mention in observation $i$:

$$Y_i = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if Rayamp Solar is recommended in cell } i \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

Share of Model Voice (SoMV):

$$\widehat{\text{SoMV}} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N Y_i$$

Weighted Visibility Index (WVI)

Logarithmic discount for recommendation position rank $r_i$ and stance factor $S_i$:

$$\text{WVI} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{S_i}{\log_2(r_i + 1)} \cdot Y_i$$

Where $S_i \in \{+1.0, +0.5, -0.5\}$ for Strongly Recommended, Neutral, or Caveated mentions.

Wilson Score 95% Confidence Interval

$$\text{CI}_{0.95} = \frac{\hat{p} + \frac{z^2}{2N} \pm z\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{N} + \frac{z^2}{4N^2}}}{1 + \frac{z^2}{N}}, \quad z = 1.96$$
